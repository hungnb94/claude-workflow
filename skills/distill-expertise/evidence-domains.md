# Evidence Domains

Read by `distill-expertise` at Step 1. A domain type is a row. To support a new kind of topic, add a row; do not add rules to `SKILL.md`.

- **Signals**: words or themes in the topic that pick this row.
- **Student evidence**: what proves that the people a teacher taught got results.
- **Proxy when none**: what to use when there are no students in the usual sense.
- **Freshness limit**: sources older than this get the `Possibly outdated` label. `Durable` means there is no limit, but the newest source still wins.
- **Apply form**: the shape of the Apply part of the report.

| Domain type | Signals | Student evidence | Proxy when none | Freshness limit | Apply form |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Investing and trading | stocks, crypto, funds, trading, real estate, financial freedom | Share of students who reached a measurable money goal (retired early, portfolio target) over years | Public track record of people who copied the method | Durable | Action checklist with a way to measure each step |
| Business and entrepreneurship | startup, sales, marketing, management, freelancing | Students or mentees who built profitable businesses or reached a revenue goal | Companies they built or advised that grew | Durable | Action checklist with a way to measure each step |
| Fitness and health | training, diet, sleep, weight, running | Clients who reached and kept a measurable body goal | Athletes or patients they coached, published outcomes | Durable | Action checklist with a way to measure each step |
| Programming and software engineering | language, architecture, testing, code quality, career growth | Mentees who became senior or staff engineers; teams they led that shipped; people who built products with their books or open source | Adoption of their open source or books in production | 3 years | Code examples, configuration, and a practice checklist |
| AI agents, LLM, and fast-moving tech | agent, LLM, prompt, RAG, model, AI coding tool | Real systems or products built with their method; followers with public, working results; benchmarks others reproduced | Their own shipped systems with public results | 18 months | Prompts, code, configuration, and a practice checklist |
| Science and research | research, academic, scientific method, papers | PhD students and postdocs who became successful researchers | Their influence on work that held up when others replicated it | Durable | Action checklist with a way to measure each step |
| Sport and competition | sport, chess, esports, competition | Athletes they coached who won at a high level | Their own results over several seasons | Durable | Training plan with a way to measure each step |
| Arts and craft | writing, music, design, drawing, photography | Students whose work was published, sold, or won awards | Their own body of work and its reception over time | Durable | Practice plan with examples |
| Languages and learning | language learning, memory, study skills, exams | Learners who reached a certified level or exam score | Published results of their method | Durable | Practice plan with a way to measure each step |
| Fallback | anything not matched above | Any measurable result of the people they taught | Their own measurable results | Durable | Action checklist with a way to measure each step |
